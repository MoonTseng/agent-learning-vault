# Show HN - Kota – Bring AI agent CLIs into the same room

> Source: [Hacker News AI Agent](https://www.kota.place/)

<p>A couple months ago, I was tired of keep copy-paste across different Agent Chatbot tabs, feeling like a slave for AI sessions, so I created Kota for my own work.<p>Kota was designed around a concept of Knights of the Round Table.<p>It treats human as part of a project team, and<p>- Runs on top of existing CLIs with existing logins, 
- Gives each agent a persisted, viewable and editable identity, instead of sessions
- Has a file-based long-term memory,
- Uses a self-managed skillset, 
- Works
